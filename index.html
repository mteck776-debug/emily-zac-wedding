Frequently asked questions

Dress code & theme

Dress up! The colour theme is earthy colours that complement burgundy and tan. The bridesmaids are wearing burgundy, so please avoid matching them. And for the ladies, no white.

Parking & accessibility

Parking is available and easy to reach, with plenty of signs to point the way.

Food & dietary requirements

Food will be canapé style, and we will make sure everyone is fed. Please add any dietary requirements when you RSVP.

Paying for meals

Meals are covered by guests at $65 per person. If you would rather not eat at the wedding, that is fine too. Bank details appear once you have sent your RSVP.
